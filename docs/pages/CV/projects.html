Probability

Student Lecture · July 2025

Martingales and Related Processes

Notes for lectures I gave as a student in a graduate stochastic processes course where students delivered the lectures. Covers martingales and their relationships to Markov, Wiener and Poisson processes, with an introduction to stochastic kernels.

Proof · Winter 2025

From the CRP to the GEM Distribution

A detailed proof that the proportions from the Chinese Restaurant Process converge in distribution to the GEM distribution.

Proof · Winter 2025

Size-Biased Permutations of the Poisson–Dirichlet Distribution

Showing that the size-biased permutation of the two-parameter Poisson–Dirichlet distribution is the two-parameter GEM distribution.
